Quick answers

Who is Rotor Run intended for?

Rotor Run is intended for players aged 13 and over, not children under 13. Store content ratings can vary by region. Parents and guardians can contact us about a child’s privacy or deletion request.

How do I control the helicopter?

Touch and hold to climb. Release to descend. Avoid the environment and incoming hazards. The takeoff prompt appears before the flight begins.

My leaderboard score hasn’t appeared.

Check that your online pilot is connected and your phone has an internet connection. Open the leaderboard and use Sync & Refresh. A successful ranked level clear can also trigger synchronization. Unverified, disconnected or failed uploads may not appear immediately.

If it still looks wrong, email your platform, pilot name, approximate flight time and a screenshot of the error. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Why is my coin bank different from the leaderboard?

The spendable balance on your device and the online verified coin record are not the same thing. The leaderboard uses server-verified earnings and recorded coin spending, rather than importing every local coin.

How do I restore Premium?

Use the app’s Restore purchases option with the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase. Store purchases and local coins are separate. Restoration between different stores or platforms is not guaranteed.

If restoration fails, send the platform and store order reference privately by email. Never send your payment-card details.

A rewarded ad didn’t grant its reward.

Rewards require the ad provider to report successful completion. If the ad fails or closes early, don’t repeatedly spend coins to work around it. Tell us your platform, app version and what happened.

The game is slow or sound is missing.

Check the app’s mute setting and your phone’s media volume. Close other demanding apps, then restart Rotor Run when you are not in a flight. If the issue repeats, send your device model, operating-system version, game version and level.

Avoid uninstalling just to troubleshoot: locally stored progress may be lost. We cannot promise recovery of unsynced local balances.

How do I delete my online pilot?

Open Pilot Profile → Delete online account and confirm. For help without the app, see our account-deletion page.

A useful bug report

Include Android or iPhone, device model, operating-system and app versions, the level or screen, and the steps that triggered the problem. Share screenshots only after removing private information.

Support enquiries are handled by email. This page has no upload form and never asks for a password.
