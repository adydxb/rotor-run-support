The short version

Rotor Run: Pixel Flight is operated by Adel Sheikh. You can play without connecting an online pilot. Online rankings, advertising and purchases involve the service providers described below. For privacy questions, contact aaentertainmentx@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Rotor Run mobile app and these support pages. It explains the data used to provide gameplay, verify scores, prevent abuse, deliver ads, validate purchases and answer support requests.

1. Progress on your device

The app saves gameplay progress, spendable coin and diamond balances, owned aircraft and cosmetics, settings and queued flights in local app storage. Local storage is not a promise that everything is never sent online: selected profile choices, ranked flight data, verified earnings and revive information are also processed online when you use connected features.

Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local progress. Device backup and restore behavior depends on the platform and app configuration. Recovery of unsynced local balances is not guaranteed.

2. Connecting a pilot and verifying flights

Online pilot connection uses Google Play Games on Android or Apple Game Center on iPhone. Our service verifies the platform’s authorization proof and stores a provider player identifier, an internal pilot identifier, the pilot name you choose, portrait, frame and optional country selection. The game’s own pilot connection does not ask for your Google or Apple password or email address.

A connected ranked flight sends information such as the level, aircraft, game rules and screen/world dimensions when requesting a flight ticket. Replay uploads contain flight timing, in-game control-change timings and revive events or methods. These are game-verification data, not a video of your screen or general tracking of touches outside the game.

Uploads happen through Sync & Refresh and can happen automatically after a successful ranked level clear, including queued earlier flights. The server replays the flight to verify scores, earned coins, recorded coin spending, playtime and level progress. A diamond-paid revive can also appear in the replay.

3. What other players can see

Leaderboard and standings information is public. It can include your pilot identifier, chosen name, portrait, frame, country, rank and ties, longest flight, coin results, verified coin bank, level progress, clears and playtime. The private platform player identifier is separate from the public pilot identifier.

Choose a pilot name that does not reveal information you want to keep private. The country flag reflects a profile selection, not permission to access your phone’s precise location.

4. Ads, purchases and service providers

Cloudflare hosts our online service and database. It processes network/request information, including IP addresses, to serve requests and protect the service. Our app uses IP-based rate limits and operational failure logs. A lack of an IP field in a pilot record does not mean infrastructure never processes an IP address. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Google AdMob provides banner and optional rewarded ads. The app requests non-personalized ads and a general-audience ad-content rating. Google’s SDK can process IP addresses, device/account identifiers, ad or app interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; IP addresses can indicate a general location. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is processed. Consent choices are requested where required and can be revisited through Pilot Profile → Ad privacy choices when available. See Google’s explanation for partner apps and Google’s privacy policy.

Apple/Google and RevenueCat handle Premium purchases and validation. RevenueCat uses an app user identifier and processes receipt or purchase-token information, entitlement status and technical app/device information to validate purchases and restore access. The current app initializes RevenueCat without a custom pilot account identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and your store’s privacy information.

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or embedded contact forms. GitHub may process normal website request information under its privacy statement. Fonts and images are served with the website, not loaded from a third-party font service.

Providers may process information outside your country. Information is shared for the functions above, support and security, or when required by applicable law; we do not sell your pilot records.

5. Retention and deletion

Your online pilot and verified records are retained to maintain your account, rankings and progress until you delete the online account. Verification replays and operational records may remain while needed for verification, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Expiration of a flight’s verification window does not guarantee immediate deletion of all replay data.

Use Pilot Profile → Delete online account to delete the online profile and associated server-side sessions, flights, replay records, score/coin records and leaderboard entries. Cached public rankings may take time to refresh. Local progress and balances are separate and are not erased by this online-account action.

Infrastructure logs, recovery copies and records independently held by stores or SDK providers follow their retention and legal requirements. We do not promise that every backup or provider-held copy disappears immediately or after a fixed number of days. For assistance or a deletion request without the app, visit account deletion or email us. Purchase records may need to be retained by your store or purchase provider.

6. Your choices and security

You can play without connecting an online pilot, change your profile choices, revisit available ad privacy choices, and request access, correction or deletion assistance by email. We may need to verify a request before changing someone’s records. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card information.

The gameplay features do not need access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise GPS location. Advertising providers can still derive a general location from network information. We use HTTPS for online services, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

If you contact support, we receive your email address, the message and any information you choose to include. We use these to handle the enquiry and keep correspondence only as needed for support, security and applicable record-keeping obligations. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child’s information or privacy concerns.

7. Intended audience: ages 13 and over

Rotor Run is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. This intended audience is separate from the content rating assigned by each store and region. Teenagers may have additional privacy rights or consent requirements under local law.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through Rotor Run, contact aaentertainmentx@gmail.com so we can investigate and assist with an appropriate deletion request. We may need to verify the request without asking for passwords or sign-in codes.
