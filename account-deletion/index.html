Account deletion

Your pilot. Your choice.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Rotor Run and go to Pilot Profile from the hangar.
  2. Select Delete online account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm only if you want to delete the online pilot.

The app needs an internet connection to complete online deletion. Disconnecting or uninstalling alone is not the same as deleting server-side records.

Can’t access the app?

Email aaentertainmentx@gmail.com with the subject Rotor Run — Account deletion request. Include your pilot name, Android or iPhone, and enough non-sensitive context to help us identify the account. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

Do not send a password, sign-in code, private key or full payment-card information. Please don’t post account details in a public GitHub issue.

Email a deletion request ↗

What online deletion removes

The online pilot profile and associated sign-in sessions, ranked flights and replays, verified score/coin records, level records and clears, and leaderboard entries in our active database. Cached standings may remain visible briefly until refreshed.

What is separate

Local balances and progress on your device are not erased by deleting the online pilot. Store purchases and RevenueCat purchase records are separate; deleting a pilot does not refund or delete a store purchase. Infrastructure logs and recovery copies follow provider retention and applicable legal requirements.

For the full explanation, read the privacy policy. We do not promise recovery of deleted online records or unsynced local progress.
